# Web research verification notes — 2026-08-08

The registry is intentionally seeded from **official authority pages** (SEC, ASIC, ACCC, ASA) plus official corporate/fund reporting hubs. It does not assert that every company publishes the same document family every year.

Important verified patterns used in the registry:

- ASIC publishes court-action releases and an infringement-notices register; infringement notice payment is not automatically an admission.
- SEC ESG enforcement releases link to administrative orders and commonly preserve settlement qualifiers.
- ASA environmental rulings provide complaint/assessment/action text suitable for exact-claim case packs.
- PETRONAS Global reporting page confirms integrated sustainability reporting and official report archive; do not accidentally substitute PETRONAS Gas/Chemicals subsidiary reports.
- Mazda official sustainability report page lists 2021–2025 directly.
- adidas official reports hub lists annual reports 2021–2025 and explains non-financial reporting is integrated into annual reports.
- Oatly official financials/filings page lists both annual and sustainability reports across the target period.
- Lufthansa official investor/reporting pages provide financial reports back to 2011 and sustainability/TCFD/SASB reporting.
- Nike official investor page provides annual reports; its official impact-report archive provides historical impact reporting.
- Fast Retailing official annual-report and sustainability-report pages provide integrated reports and sustainability data.
- PVH official investor/SEC and responsibility pages separate annual filings from corporate-responsibility reports.

Any URL that later moves must be rediscovered from the same official domain and changed in the registry with a changelog entry.
